Tailoring rules

BD/Publishing: lead with [US CPI range], [D1/D7/D30 range], creative learnings, build readiness, UA repeatability, roadmap, and what support is needed.

Investment/Corp Dev: lead with validated US signals, cash-flow timing, team efficiency, IP/category option value, and flexible structures such as asset sale, project transfer, strategic investment, or acquisition.

Founder/CEO: keep it short, mention why their portfolio specifically fits, and ask who should review the teaser.
