# 日韩游戏发行/投资买方外联话术包

生成日期：2026-06-02

## 使用方式

第一轮建议只发 A-tier 中最强的 10 家，观察打开率、回复方向和对方最关心的数据，再微调剩余 A/B tier 的措辞。首封邮件可以实名披露公司与游戏名，但不要在冷邮件里放完整原始 cohort、财务细节、代码、未公开 build 或 cap table。

## 首封英文邮件

Subject: US-tested casual adventure/simulation game - publishing or strategic partnership fit?

```text
Hi [Name],

I'm [Your Name] from [StudioLegalName]. We are building [GameName], a mobile casual adventure/simulation game in the Family Island neighborhood, and we have completed several US market tests with encouraging CPI and retention signals.

We are now speaking with a small number of Japanese and Korean publishers/investors that can help us scale, invest, acquire the project/team, or structure a publishing/MG partnership. Based on [Company]'s work in [relevant portfolio / casual / mobile / global publishing], I thought this may be worth a short conversation.

Happy to share a compact deck, gameplay video, and KPI summary covering CPI, D1/D7/D30, test spend, creative learnings, monetization assumptions, and roadmap.

Would you be open to a 30-minute intro call next week?

Best,
[Signature]
```

## 三封跟进邮件

### Follow-up 1：3-4 个工作日后

```text
Hi [Name], quick follow-up in case this is relevant for [Company]'s mobile publishing or investment team.

The short version: [GameName] has completed US market tests with encouraging CPI/retention signals, and we are looking for a partner who can help scale or discuss investment/acquisition options. I can send a 1-page teaser first if easier.

Would [alternate contact/team] be the right person to review?
```

### Follow-up 2：再过 7-10 天

```text
Hi [Name], I don't want to clutter your inbox. For [Company], would this be better routed to publishing/BD, corporate development, or investment?

We can share the deck and KPI summary under a simple NDA if needed. The opportunity is flexible: publishing/MG, strategic investment, or acquisition discussion.
```

### Follow-up 3：最终收口

```text
Hi [Name], closing the loop for now. If [Company] is currently reviewing external mobile casual titles or strategic investments, I would be happy to reconnect and send the KPI teaser.

Thanks for considering it.
```

## 按对象微调

- BD/Publishing：强调美国测试、CPI、留存、创意素材学习、可复制 UA、版本节奏和发行需要的支持。
- Investment/Corp Dev：强调品类空间、团队效率、已验证市场信号、资金缺口、可选交易结构和清晰 diligence 包。
- CEO/Founder：强调时间窗口、现金流压力下的灵活交易结构、团队愿景和对方战略协同。

## 对方回复后需要准备

1-page teaser、完整 Deck、2 分钟 gameplay 视频、CPI/D1/D7/D30 cohort、渠道/素材花费、留存曲线、变现假设、12 个月 roadmap、团队履历、期望交易结构、NDA、APK/TestFlight/build 链接。
